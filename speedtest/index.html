How to read your results
  • Download: how fast you receive data (streaming, browsing). 25 Mbps or more handles 4K video.
  • Upload: how fast you send data (video calls, sending files). 5 Mbps or more is fine for video calls.
  • Ping: delay in milliseconds. Lower is better. Under 50 ms feels instant, and over 150 ms makes calls and games lag.
  • Jitter: how much the ping jumps around. Under 30 ms keeps calls smooth.
  • Wi‑Fi or internet? Test next to the router and in the slow room. If both are slow, the problem is your internet plan or provider. If only the far room is slow, the problem is Wi‑Fi coverage.
  • Local network mode (with server.py) skips the internet entirely and measures your Wi‑Fi alone.
Internet tests use Cloudflare's public speed test service. Each test uses roughly 50–500 MB of data, depending on your speed.
